About Hameed Brothers

Founded in 1985, Hameed Brothers Pesticides has been at the forefront of agricultural innovation, providing farmers with premium quality pesticides and agricultural chemicals that ensure healthy crops and maximum yields.

Our commitment to excellence and sustainable farming practices has made us a trusted partner for thousands of farmers across the region. We combine traditional agricultural wisdom with modern scientific approaches.
